This is the accompanying website of my book:

> **Rebora, Simone.** 2026. Computational Literary Studies: Theory and Methods. Routledge.

More info on the book is available ***by clicking on its cover*** or at [this link](https://www.routledge.com/Computational-Literary-Studies-Theory-and-Methods/Rebora/p/book/9781041059769).

To explore the website, ***just click on the chapter titles here below***. Each page contains a synthetic list of tools and resources connected to the chapter's contents.  

## Chapters

### [Introduction](Introduction/)  
The introduction of the book focusses on the main concepts in computational literary studies and offers a brief history of the field. The accompanying page contains a list of main research and infrastructure projects.
### [1. Literary historiography and computational text analysis](TextAnalysis/)  
The first chapter presents the main theories of literary historiography and connects them to the most basic approaches in computational text analysis. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources for natural language processing.
### [2. Stylistics and stylometry](Stylometry/)  
The second chapter discusses the concept of style in stylistics and stylometry. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources in stylometry.
### [3. Emotions in literature and sentiment analysis](SentimentAnalysis/)  
The third chapter focusses on emotions in literary studies and in sentiment analysis. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources for sentiment analysis.
### [4. Thematic criticism and topic modeling](TopicModeling/)  
The fourth chapter explores the connections between thematic criticism and topic modeling. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources for topic modeling.
### [5. Mapping literature](Mapping/)  
The fifth chapter focusses on GIS technology and spatial literary studies. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources for mapping.
### [6. Networking characters](Networks/)  
The sixth chapter connects character studies and social network analysis. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources for network analysis.
### [7. Intertextuality and text reuse detection](TextReuse/)  
The seventh chapter focusses on the issue of intertextuality and its study through computational tools. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources for text reuse detection and semantic similarity.
### [8. Artificial Intelligence and the challenge of interpretation](MLGenAI/)  
The eight chapter discusses the challenge of interpretation in computational literary studies. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources for machine learning and generative artificial intelligence.
### [Conclusion](Conclusion/)  
The conclusion reconnects the topics discussed in all chapters through the concept of modeling. The accompanying page contains a list of networking initiatives relevant to Computational Literary Studies.
