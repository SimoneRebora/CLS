The first chapter presents the main theories of literary historiography and connects them to the most basic approaches in computational text analysis. The accompanying page contains a list of tools and resources for natural language processing.

## Text Analysis Platforms

### [Voyant tools](https://voyant-tools.org/)

![Screenshot of Voyant tools]({{ '/assets/images/TextAnalysis_1.png' | relative_url }})

Voyant Tools is a web-based reading and analysis environment for digital texts.  
***More info [here](https://voyant-tools.org/)***

### [AntConc](https://www.laurenceanthony.net/software/antconc/)

![Screenshot of AntConc]({{ '/assets/images/TextAnalysis_2.png' | relative_url }})

A freeware corpus analysis toolkit for concordancing and text analysis  
***More info [here](https://www.laurenceanthony.net/software/antconc/)***

### [TXM](https://txm.gitpages.huma-num.fr/textometrie/en/)

![Screenshot of TXM]({{ '/assets/images/TextAnalysis_3.png' | relative_url }})

Textometry, born in France in the 80’s, has developed powerful techniques for the analysis of large body of texts. Following lexicometry and text statistical analysis, it offers tools and methods tested in multiple branches of the humanities and is statistically well founded. The project brings together open-source Textometry software developments to set up a modular platform called TXM.   
***More info [here](https://txm.gitpages.huma-num.fr/textometrie/en/)***

### [Sketch Engine](https://www.sketchengine.eu/)

![Screenshot of Sketch Engine]({{ '/assets/images/TextAnalysis_7.png' | relative_url }})

A commercial software to study text corpora. Its algorithms analyze authentic texts totalling billions of words (text corpora) to identify instantly what is typical in language and what is rare, unusual, or emerging. It is also designed for text analysis or text mining applications.  
***More info [here](https://www.sketchengine.eu/)***

## NLP Tools

### [Udpipe](https://lindat.mff.cuni.cz/services/udpipe/)

![Screenshot of Udpipe]({{ '/assets/images/TextAnalysis_4.png' | relative_url }})

UDPipe is a trainable pipeline for tokenization, tagging, lemmatization and dependency parsing of CoNLL-U files. UDPipe is language-agnostic and can be trained given annotated data in CoNLL-U format. Trained models are provided for nearly all UD treebanks. UDPipe is available as a binary for Linux/Windows/OS X, as a library for C++, Python, Perl, Java, C#, and as a web service. Third-party R CRAN package also exists.  
***More info [here](https://lindat.mff.cuni.cz/services/udpipe/)***

### [spaCy](https://spacy.io/)

![Screenshot of spaCy]({{ '/assets/images/TextAnalysis_5.png' | relative_url }})

spaCy is a free, open-source library for advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) in Python.  
***More info [here](https://spacy.io/)***

### [Stanza](https://stanfordnlp.github.io/stanza/)

![Screenshot of Stanza]({{ '/assets/images/TextAnalysis_6.png' | relative_url }})

Stanza is a collection of accurate and efficient tools for the linguistic analysis of many human languages. Starting from raw text, Stanza divides it into sentences and words, and then can recognize parts of speech and entities, do syntactic analysis, and more. Stanza brings state-of-the-art NLP models to languages of your choosing.  
***More info [here](https://stanfordnlp.github.io/stanza/)***
