*Last update: 2026-08-02*

The introduction of the book focusses on the main concepts in computational literary studies and offers a brief history of the field. The accompanying page contains a list of main research and infrastructure projects.

## Computational Literary Studies Projects

### [Distant Reading for European Literary History](https://www.distant-reading.net/)

![Screenshot of Distant Reading for European Literary History]({{ '/assets/images/Introduction_1.png' | relative_url }})

A network of researchers jointly developing the resources and methods for the computational study of European literary history.  
***Funded by:*** European Commission  
***Duration:*** 2017-2022  
***More info [here](https://www.distant-reading.net/)***

### [SPP (Schwerpunktprogramme) 2207 Computational Literary Studies](https://dfg-spp-cls.github.io/)

![Screenshot of SPP (Schwerpunktprogramme) 2207 Computational Literary Studies]({{ '/assets/images/Introduction_2.png' | relative_url }})

A priority programme dedicated to developig and establishing innovative computational methods in the field of literary studies.  
***Funded by:*** German Research Foundation (DFG)  
***Duration:*** 2019-present  
***More info [here](https://dfg-spp-cls.github.io/)***

### [Computational Literary Studies Infrastructure (CLS INFRA)](https://clsinfra.io/)

![Screenshot of Computational Literary Studies Infrastructure (CLS INFRA)]({{ '/assets/images/Introduction_3.png' | relative_url }})

A four-year partnership to build a shared resource of high-quality data, tools and knowledge to aid new approaches to studying literature in the digital age.   
***Funded by:*** European Commission  
***Duration:*** 2021-2025  
***More info [here](https://clsinfra.io/)***
